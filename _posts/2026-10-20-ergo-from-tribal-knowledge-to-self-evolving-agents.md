## Summary

On the knowledge an insurer runs on that was never written down anywhere — the
kind carried in conversations, habits and individual experience — and on whether
self-evolving agents can capture it, govern it and make it reusable across the
organisation. The framing is demographic as much as technical: a large share of
the sector's most experienced staff is near retirement, and undocumented
knowledge leaves with them.
